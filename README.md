#Wetnose Animal Rescue centre
The organisation has been rescuing animals and finding them homes since 1999. It cares of more than 600 animals until suitable homes can be found, regardless how long it may take. The organisation focuses on rescuing, rehabilitating, and rehoming abandoned, abused, and neglected animals.
The reason I chose this organisation is because I really love animals. What motivated me further was the belief that animals have emotions like us humans. For example, like us humans, animals will seek help when they are in danger and need a safe place to recover  emotionally and physically.
https://www.wetnose.co.za/
